# Face Mesh

## Register

product

## Users
Creators, developers, and curious visitors using a laptop or phone camera. They want an immediate, playful demonstration of browser-based computer vision without installing software or uploading video.

## Product Purpose
Face Mesh recognizes six deliberate poses and expressions in real time, maps them to visual and audio reactions, and reveals the relevant runtime code path as it executes. Success means a first-time visitor can grant camera access, understand how to frame themselves, trigger a reaction within seconds, and see why it happened.

## Brand Personality
Forensic, kinetic, transparent. The experience should feel like a machine with its shell removed: technical enough for developers, but legible and playful for everyone else.

## Anti-references
Do not resemble a generic SaaS dashboard, a novelty beauty filter, fake "Matrix rain," or a terminal full of meaningless random logs. Avoid decorative cards and cyberpunk glow applied without functional meaning.

## Design Principles
1. Show the mechanism, not just the result.
2. Camera and recognition remain the primary interaction.
3. Every visual pulse must correspond to a real runtime event.
4. Teach framing and recover clearly when tracking is uncertain.
5. Keep camera processing private and on-device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard-visible focus, sufficient contrast, readable status text, and 44px controls. Respect reduced-motion preferences and never rely on color alone to communicate recognition state. Audio is optional and muted by default.
